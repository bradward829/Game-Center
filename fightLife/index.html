Instructions:

You must make it to level 5 without using all 5 of your lives to successfully win the game.

-Use the keys W, A, S, and D to launch your attack against your crazed opponent.
-W will kick, A will block, S will throw left punch, and D will throw a right punch.
-Use the arrow keys Up to jump, and Left and Right arrows to move accordingly.
-The levels get harder as you go
-Most importantly, be ready when you push that Start button!!!
