Rules and Instructions:

On the first roll:

A 7 or 11 is a winner. A 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, or 10 will be your point. A 2, 3, or 12 are only relevant on before you have a point and they are "Craps" (aka: you lose your bet).

After the first roll:

You must try to "roll your point" before you roll a 7 which is now "Craps".

Some things to keep in mind are:

You can not roll until you have placed a bet.

Some points are easier to hit than others so play accordingly!!
