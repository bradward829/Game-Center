## **User Experience**

Upon completion of the Instructions for Opening/Viewing printed above, the user may feel free to enjoy my **Capstone Project**. If you click on Dynamic Dice these are the **Rules and Instructions for Dynamic Dice:**

On the first roll:
A 7 or 11 is a winner. A 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, or 10 will be your point. A 2, 3, or 12 are only relevant on before you have a point and they are "Craps" (aka: you lose your bet).

After the first roll:
You must try to "roll your point" before you roll a 7 which is now "Craps".

Some things to keep in mind are:
You can not roll until you have placed a bet.

Some points are easier to hit than others so play accordingly!!

Lastly, this game is only for fun and not intended to promote or endorse gambling!

If you click on Fight Life these are the 

**Rules and Instructions for Fight Life:**

You must make it to level 5 without using all 5 of your lives to successfully win the game.
The levels get harder as you progress.
Use the keys W, A, S, and D to launch your attack against your crazed opponent.
W will kick, A will block, S will throw left punch, and D will throw a right punch.
Use the arrow keys Up to jump, and Left and Right arrows to move accordingly.
You can use the Pause button to pause game or the Restart button to restart if you need to.
If you need the rules at any time you can pause the game to reveal instructions button link.
Most importantly, be ready when you push that Start button!!!
